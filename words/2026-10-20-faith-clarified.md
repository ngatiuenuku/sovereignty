# 20 Oct 2026: Faith, as I see it

The Joseph story, Black Nobility, the Josephites and Josephine all stay. They are the only thing I have had to hold on to for hope, along with my missing testicle, which I can laugh about.

I don't share the views of others on Jesus, and I can't stand much of what is done in his name. My only problem is with the hate and harm that have been done under Christianity and Catholicism. It shows how easily a story can be bent, and I include myself in that.

I don't want my wrongdoings, my vivid dreams or my imagination left out. They have been vital for my own accountability, even if, looking back, I feel I have been acting in self-defence my whole life.

Freedom of speech is real and can be measured here on earth. As I see it, Jesus is something each person who calls on him needs to experience for themselves in some way. Otherwise the teaching is a waste of time.

"Gee is us": to me this means all humans on earth joined as one. Our DNA is all one, except for those who carry something extra, and they get a bit more than the average Joe. Even so, throwing stones always has consequences, especially from inside glass houses.
