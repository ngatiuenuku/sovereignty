# Sovereignty

A personal project of Joseph Bristol (GitHub: ngatiuenuku).

## Rules

- **For Joseph only.** This project is about his own life, as an individual.
- **His words are the only source.** Nothing goes in here except Joseph's own words.
- **No one else's input.** No other person's content, opinions or contributions may be added. Pull requests and outside contributions will not be accepted.
- **Open to all after he is gone.** Anyone may read it, fork it, or carry it on after Joseph is gone, as long as his words are kept as his.

## Lane

There are two parts, and they have equal weight.

1. **Sovereignty.** Joseph's own independence and self-determination as an individual. That covers:
   - self-reliance in income, food, housing, energy and technology, including offline AI;
   - whakapapa, hapū and tikanga-based identity;
   - private arrangements he controls.

   It is not about helping others exit the system. It does not deal with Crown agencies or Crown corporations, because their constitutions conflict with his beliefs.

2. **Spirit.** Joseph's spiritual side, on his own terms. That covers wairua, karakia, whakapapa and tūpuna, his faith and reflections, and the practices he chooses.

## Scope

This project is separate from Joseph's case work with ACC, MSD, the courts, police and the IPCA, Health NZ, historic claims, and the Crown. It does not contain or handle any personal case matters.

## Structure

- `words/`: Joseph's own words, kept as he gave them.
- `*-clarified.md`: tidied versions of Joseph's words, written for clarity and peace; the originals stay alongside.
